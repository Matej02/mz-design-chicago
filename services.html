What We Do

Services built around how you actually live.

Three connected disciplines — interior design, bathrooms & outdoor living, and decor coordination — delivered as one project, one point of contact, one cohesive result.

01 / Complete Interior Design

Every room, considered as one home.

Kitchens, living rooms, home offices, children's rooms, bedrooms, and bathrooms — we design the layout, finishes, lighting, furniture, and styling together, so nothing feels bolted on after the fact.

  • — Space planning & furniture layout
  • — Material, finish & color palettes
  • — Lighting design (ambient, task & accent)
  • — Furniture selection & styling
02 / Bathrooms + Outdoor Living

Private retreats, indoors and out.

Distinctive bathrooms with thoughtfully selected fixtures — including premium brands such as Kohler — plus porches, patios, and gardens designed as genuine outdoor rooms, not an afterthought off the back door.

  • — Spa-style bathroom design & fixture selection
  • — Patios, pergolas & outdoor lounges
  • — Landscape-adjacent garden styling
  • — Outdoor lighting & fire features

Fixture availability, including Kohler selections, may vary by supplier and lead time.

03 / Decor + Design Coordination

The details that make it feel finished.

Seasonal and wedding décor, custom handmade details, sourcing, and a connected path to qualified trade professionals when your project needs licensed plumbing or electrical work.

  • — Seasonal & event styling (see Celebrations)
  • — Custom, handmade signature details
  • — Material & furnishing sourcing
  • — Coordination with licensed, insured trades

How Budget-Conscious Luxury Works

Where the savings actually come from.

M&Z is a design and coordination studio, not a general contractor with in-house overhead to cover on every job. That structure is what lets us pass real savings through — up to 40% versus traditional full-service remodeling, depending on scope, material selections, and trade requirements.

Strategic material sourcing

We know where to spend on a statement material and where a smart alternative reads just as well.

Licensed, insured trade partners

Plumbing and electrical work is coordinated with vetted independent professionals — priced for the scope, not a markup on staff you don't need.

One coordinated plan

Design and trade work move on the same schedule, which avoids the rework and delays that quietly inflate a budget.

Let's scope your project.

Share the rooms or spaces you're thinking about and we'll follow up with next steps and a realistic budget range.
