About M&Z

Design that starts with how you live.

M&Z Design Chicago pairs interior design with construction coordination, so the vision and the build speak the same language from the very first sketch.

Our Philosophy

Personalized. Practical. Handmade where it counts.

We don't work from a catalog of showroom looks. Every project starts with your space, your habits, and a realistic budget — then we select materials strategically, design memorable custom moments, and bring in licensed, insured trade professionals only where a project genuinely needs them.

It's the same instinct that leads us to hand-make original sculptural pieces for a room rather than order another catalog fixture — a belief that a finished space should have at least one detail nobody else's home has.

Clear From The Beginning

One vision. Four steps.

Every project — from a single room to a full outdoor retreat — moves through the same four stages.

Discover

We learn your space, priorities, taste, and a realistic investment range.

Design

You receive a cohesive visual concept, material direction, and a practical plan.

Source

We help identify beautiful materials — and smart alternatives — at the right price.

Coordinate

Once selections are made, design and qualified trade work move forward as one coordinated project.

How We Work With Trades

We coordinate. Licensed professionals build.

M&Z designs and project-manages; we are not ourselves a plumbing or electrical contractor. When a project needs that work, we coordinate with vetted, independently licensed and insured trade professionals, and stay involved to make sure the finished result matches the design.

What Design Actually Means Here

You get drawings, not just mood boards.

Every project is documented properly — floor plans, elevations, fixture details and a written materials schedule.

Why it matters

Your trades quote from the same drawing you approved, so the finished room matches the room you signed off on — no mid-build guesswork, no surprise change orders because something was never specified.

What the drawing set covers

  • Floor plan with dimensions
  • Vanity and cabinetry elevations
  • Fixture, sink, mirror and lighting details
  • Written notes and materials schedule
