M&Z Original

Ideas shaped into experience.

M&Z Design explores more than rooms. These original concepts combine sculptural form, light, water, and responsive technology — objects that change how a space feels, each finished with a signature brushed-gold M&Z maker's plaque.

01 / Connected Living

The Musical Waterfall Chair

A one-of-a-kind sculptural chair envisioned with an integrated waterfall. Connected by Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, the water feature responds when music plays from your phone — turning sound, movement, and light into a calming sensory experience.

M&Z Original · Hand-finished
02 / Outdoor Living Art

Nature in a New Form

An illuminated sculptural vessel combined with natural branches — a functional outdoor art piece built around concrete, nature, light, and harmony.

Original prototype by Martina Dudek

03 / Functional Art

The Branch Light

A one-of-a-kind illuminated natural-branch towel holder — function reimagined as sculpture, warm-lit from within against a hand-textured wall.

M&Z Original · Hand-finished
04 / From Inspiration to Invention

A Light Becomes a Chair

The fluid transparency of this glass pendant became the starting point for an entirely new seating concept. M&Z translated its luminous, teardrop form into sculptural furniture — weightless in appearance, built to be an everyday centerpiece.
