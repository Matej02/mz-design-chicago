The Signature

A mark made to last.

Original M&Z pieces are finished with a brushed-gold maker's plaque in durable 316L stainless steel with a PVD gold finish — designed to hold its shine in distinctive indoor and outdoor settings alike.

Every M&Z original piece can carry a signature designer's plaque — a discreet mark of its custom design. You choose where it appears and select a finish that complements your space, from brushed gold and champagne gold to copper, nickel, or a custom option.
