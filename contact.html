Before You Ask

Straight answers.

The things people most often want to know before they get in touch.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes — a 15-minute discovery call costs nothing and carries no obligation. It's there so we can both work out whether the project is a fit.

In-home consultations and written design recommendations are paid services. We'll tell you which one you need and what it costs before you commit to anything.

Do you carry out the construction work yourselves?

No. M&Z is a design and coordination studio, not a general contractor. Plumbing and electrical work is carried out by licensed, insured independent trade professionals, and we coordinate them against the approved drawings.

What does “save up to 40%” actually cover?

It applies to select materials, fixtures and décor, through professional sourcing, vendor relationships and special purchasing opportunities — products at prices below comparable retail.

It does not apply to whole-renovation costs or to labor. Availability and savings vary by brand, product and project.

What do I actually receive?

A documented drawing set: floor plan with dimensions, cabinetry and vanity elevations, fixture, sink, mirror and lighting details, plus written notes and a materials schedule — so your trades quote from the same drawing you approved.

Which areas do you work in?

Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Tell us where the project is when you get in touch and we'll confirm on the first call.

Can I commission one of the original pieces on its own?

Yes. The sculptural originals — the illuminated vessels, the branch towel holder, the seating concepts — can be commissioned directly, with or without a wider design project. Each can be finished with the brushed-gold M&Z maker's plaque.

Are the images on this site finished projects?

They are design visualizations and original concepts, and every image is labelled as such in the portfolio. We'd rather show you clearly what something is than let a render pass for a photograph.
