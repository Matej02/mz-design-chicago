Privacy Policy

A short, plain-language account of what happens to your information when you use this site. Last updated September 2026.

What we collect

When you submit the consultation form on the Contact page, we collect whatever you enter: your name, your preferred phone or email, and any project details you share — type of project, rooms, budget range, timeline, and your message. We don't collect anything beyond what that form asks for, and we don't require an account or login anywhere on this site.

How it's used

Submitting the form sends its contents directly to the studio's inbox by email, so M&Z can respond to your inquiry. That's the only use — your information isn't added to a mailing list, used for advertising, or sold or shared with anyone for marketing purposes.

Who it passes through

Two outside services are involved in running this site, each handling a narrow, specific job:

  • Resend — the email delivery service that carries your consultation request from this website to the studio's inbox. Resend processes the message only to deliver it and doesn't use it for any purpose of its own.
  • Google Analytics — used to understand overall site traffic: which pages are visited, roughly how visitors found the site, and general device and location information (city-level, not precise). This is aggregate usage data, not tied to your name or contact details from the form. You can opt out at any time using your browser's privacy settings or an ad-blocking / tracking-protection extension.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets a small number of cookies to distinguish visits. This site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and nothing here is sold to ad networks or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Consultation requests stay in the studio's email inbox for as long as is useful for following up on the project — there's no separate database or CRM behind this site.

Your choices

You can ask what information we have tied to your inquiry, or ask us to delete it, at any time — just email mzdesign14@yahoo.com.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out the same way: mzdesign14@yahoo.com or +1 773-968-1042.
